An open `fix` entry holds its carried routes on legacy (AAP 0.4.2, 0.7.5). SEC-04 (`?appreload`, which never cuts over) and SEC-08 to SEC-11 have one. Those of SEC-08 to SEC-10 hold `admin:cusers.addtogroup`, `admin:cusers.removefromgroup`, `admin:cusers.edituser` and `admin:ceditprofile.update`, writes on `grp:identity.credential` units, as `legacy-write` entries in every cut-over version, so they stay on legacy until the user accepts; the reads they name stay `legacy`. SEC-11's entry names reads only, which stay `legacy`. SEC-01 to SEC-03, SEC-05 and SEC-07 carry no fix entry, so their carried routes can move with their groups or units unless a halted entry holds them; SEC-02's `admin:cchangesets.publish` is withheld (decision list), a `legacy-write` entry in every version. Carried routes declare `config.opt2.public: true` or the exact legacy role, keeping deny-by-default explicit.
